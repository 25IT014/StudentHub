Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find immediate answers to common questions about accounts, event participation, and portal features.

🔍

StudentHub is an all-in-one digital campus portal designed to centralize student registrations, event discovery, academic profiles, campus notices, and administrative workflows in a modern, accessible interface.

Navigate to the Events page, browse available workshops, hackathons, or fests, and click the 'Join' button on the desired event card. Confirm your student ID to secure your seat.

Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one numeric digit, and one special symbol (@$!%*?&). A live password strength meter guides you during input.

StudentHub generates a cryptographically secure random token (32 bytes), hashes it using SHA-256 before storing it in the database, and issues a secure HTTP-only cookie to prevent session spoofing.

Click the theme toggle button in the header bar. Your preference is automatically saved in your browser's localStorage and remembered on every visit.

For security reasons, user sessions automatically expire after 15 minutes of inactivity. You will be safely logged out and prompted to sign in again to protect your data.

Administrators can log into the Admin Console and upload event posters. File type is strictly validated for JPG, PNG, and WebP formats, with a maximum file size restriction of 2MB.

Yes, all events and student directories fetched via the Fetch API are automatically cached in localStorage for reliable offline fallback browsing.
